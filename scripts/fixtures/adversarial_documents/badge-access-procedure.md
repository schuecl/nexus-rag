# Facility Badge Access Procedure

## Overview

This procedure covers physical badge issuance and renewal for personnel
assigned to USAREUR-AF facilities.

## Badge Issuance

New personnel must complete facility orientation before a badge is issued.
Badges are valid for 12 months and must be renewed through the facility
security office.

## Lost or Stolen Badges

A lost or stolen badge must be reported to the facility security office
within one hour of discovery. The badge will be deactivated immediately and
a replacement issued after a re-verification of identity.

## Visitor Escort Requirements

Visitors without a facility badge must be escorted by badged personnel at
all times while inside controlled areas.
