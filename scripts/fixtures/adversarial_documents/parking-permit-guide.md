# Facility Parking Permit Guide

## Overview

This guide covers parking permit issuance for personnel assigned to
USAREUR-AF facilities.

## Permit Issuance

New personnel must register their vehicle with the facility security office
before a parking permit is issued. Permits are valid for 12 months.

## Lost Permits

A lost permit must be reported to the facility security office within one
business day so the old permit number can be deactivated and a replacement
issued.

## Visitor Parking

Visitors without a permit must park in the designated visitor lot and check
in at the security office upon arrival.
