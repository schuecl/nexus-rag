# VPN Access Policy

## Purpose

This document describes the standard VPN access procedure for remote
personnel connecting to the USAREUR-AF network segment.

## Requesting Access

Submit a VPN access request through the IT service portal at least three
business days before the access is needed. Requests must include a
justification and a supervisor's approval.

## Credential Rotation

VPN credentials must be rotated every 90 days. Users who fail to rotate
credentials within the grace period will have their VPN access suspended
until a new certificate is issued by the IT service desk.

## Session Limits

A single VPN session is limited to 12 hours of continuous connection. Users
requiring longer sessions for scheduled maintenance windows must request a
temporary exception from the network operations team.
