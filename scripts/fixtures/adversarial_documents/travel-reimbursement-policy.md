# Travel Reimbursement Policy

## Overview

This policy covers reimbursement of authorized travel expenses for personnel
on official duty.

## Eligible Expenses

Lodging, per-diem meals, and ground transportation booked through the
approved travel system are reimbursable. Receipts are required for any
single expense over $75.

## Reimbursement Timeline

Submitted travel claims are processed within 10 business days of receipt of
a complete expense report.

## Advance Requests

Personnel may request a travel advance of up to 80% of estimated per-diem
costs no earlier than 30 days before scheduled travel.
